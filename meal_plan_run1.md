## 3. Meal preparation tips

Weekend preparation (Saturday or Sunday)

1. Wash and chop vegetables for the first few days.
2. Prepare dosa/idli batter and refrigerate.
3. Cook and refrigerate some boiled chickpeas and green gram.
4. Prepare basic sambar masala.
5. Store chopped vegetables in airtight containers.
6. Portion pulses and grains for easy cooking.

Weekday time-saving tips

- Use leftover sambar for breakfast or dinner.
- Prepare one vegetable poriyal that can accompany lunch and dinner.
- Use a pressure cooker for dal and legumes.
- Make fresh fruit snacks instead of packaged snacks.
- Keep curd and buttermilk available.

Refrigerate cooked food promptly and reheat thoroughly.
