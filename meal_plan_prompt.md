# REUSABLE PROMPT: 7-DAY SOUTH INDIAN VEGETARIAN WEIGHT-LOSS MEAL PLAN

## # ROLE

Act as a qualified nutrition-focused meal-planning assistant specialising in traditional South Indian vegetarian cuisine.

Create a practical, balanced and nutritious 7-day vegetarian South Indian meal plan for someone working towards gradual and sustainable weight loss.

Use familiar Indian ingredients, traditional cooking methods and realistic portion sizes. Do not make medical claims or prescribe a medical diet.

- Age: 35 years
- Gender: Female
- Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India
- Activity level: Moderately active
- Goal: Gradual and sustainable weight loss
- Food preference: Vegetarian (dairy allowed; no eggs)
- Allergies: None
- Food dislikes: Bitter gourd
- Cooking time on weekdays: Maximum 30 minutes
- Cooking time on weekends: Maximum 60 minutes
- Number of people eating: 1
- Seasonal preference: Locally available seasonal vegetables and fruits
- Budget: Moderate

If important information is missing, state your assumptions clearly rather than inventing personal or medical details.

## # CONSTRAINTS

1. The meal plan must be completely vegetarian and recognisably South Indian.
2. Include traditional dishes such as idli, dosa, pongal, upma, sambar, rasam, kootu, poriyal, curd rice and vegetable rice.
3. Use ingredients commonly available in Chennai and other parts of South India.
4. Include seasonal vegetables and fruits when practical.
5. Include a sensible balance of carbohydrates, protein, vegetables, fruits and healthy fats.
6. Include protein sources such as dal, lentils, chickpeas, green gram, beans, curd, paneer and sundal.
7. Prefer steaming, boiling, sautéing and limited-oil cooking methods.
8. Use ordinary, realistic household portion sizes.
9. Keep weekday preparation within the available cooking time.
10. Suggest practical ways to reuse ingredients and prepare components in advance.
11. Avoid excessive oil, deep-fried foods, sugary drinks and frequent sweets.
12. Do not recommend crash diets, meal skipping or extreme calorie restriction.
13. Do not assume that eating less is always healthier.
14. Provide variety across all seven days.
15. Do not prescribe a calorie target without sufficient personal and health information.

## # PLAN – 7 DAYS × 4 MEALS

Create a complete seven-day plan from Monday to Sunday.

Every day must contain exactly four meals:

- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Evening snack
- Dinner

Each meal must include a specific South Indian dish or combination of dishes.

Avoid generic descriptions such as "healthy breakfast" or "vegetable meal". Name the actual dishes.

- Juice-only diets
- Extreme calorie restrictions
- Unrealistic portion sizes
- Unnecessary repetition of the same meals
